Sign in with the app

Open Unitool on your phone, go to Settings → Website, tap Scan QR code to sign in and point the camera at this code. The phone shows this browser and asks you to confirm.

What signing in does

  • This browser becomes known as your installation of Unitool — not as you. There is no name, no email and no password anywhere in it.
  • The documentation of the hidden parts you have opened in the app appears in the sidebar. Open another one on the phone, and its pages appear here too.
  • The server notes when this browser opens a page and which page, and keeps that for 90 days.
  • Sign out under Account, or on the phone in Settings → Website.
