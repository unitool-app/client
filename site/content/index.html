Small tools in one app. No ads, no account, no tracking.

A calculator, a currency converter and a two-factor code generator that open straight onto what you came for. Your arithmetic stays on your phone, and your 2FA keys are locked in its secure hardware.

Android and iOS, from one Kotlin codebase that also runs on the desktop and in the browser.

Features

Unitool is a host for small apps. Home holds the ones you put there; the rest wait in the catalogue under “+”.

Calculator

Four functions, percent the way a pocket calculator reads it, chained sums, and results that say 0.3 when you add 0.1 and 0.2.

Currency converter

Over 160 currencies from published rate tables. The last table is kept, so it still answers with no signal.

2FA codes

Time-based codes from a QR code, a link or a typed key. Import from Google Authenticator; export encrypted to your GitHub SSH keys.

App lock

Apps that hold secrets open only with your fingerprint, face or screen lock, and lock again when you leave. On Android they are also kept out of screenshots and the recent-apps preview.

Principles

  • Nothing you type leaves the phone. No analytics, no crash reporter, no advertising ID, no sign-in. The privacy policy lists every request the app makes.
  • Secrets live in secure hardware. 2FA keys are encrypted under a key held by Android's Keystore or the iPhone's Secure Enclave, which cannot be read out.
  • Open formats, no lock-in. otpauth:// links, RFC 6238 codes, age files and OpenSSH keys — readable by tools that are not ours.
  • Quiet by default. No notification permission, no push registration, no Firebase until you use a tool that needs them.

Questions

Is Unitool free?

Yes. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscription.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in of any kind. The app works the moment it opens.

What happens to my 2FA keys if I lose my phone?

The keys on that phone cannot be read without unlocking it, and they cannot be copied off it. To be able to restore them, make an encrypted export to your GitHub SSH keys while you still have the phone. How it works.

Does it work offline?

Everything except fetching new exchange rates. The converter keeps the last table it downloaded and uses it when there is no connection.
