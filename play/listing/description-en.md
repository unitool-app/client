## Full description (4000 max)

```
Unitool is a calculator, and only a calculator.

It opens straight onto the keypad. There is no splash to sit through, no account to make, no
banner across the bottom and no permission dialog on first run. The answer is the largest thing
on the screen, because it is the thing you came for.

WHAT IT DOES

• Add, subtract, multiply, divide
• Percent, read the way a pocket calculator reads it — 200 + 10% adds ten percent of the 200
• Sign flip, backspace, clear
• Chained sums left to right, so 2 + 3 × 4 keeps going without you re-entering anything
• Results rounded so that 0.1 + 0.2 reads as 0.3, not as the binary truth about it

SETTINGS, AND THERE ARE THREE

• Language — English or Russian, switched instantly, no restart
• Vibration — the short tap under each key, on or off
• The build number, for when something needs reporting

PRIVACY

Unitool has no network permission. It cannot send anything anywhere, and it does not try. There
is no analytics, no crash reporter, no advertising ID and no account. The only things it writes
down are the two settings above, in the app's own private storage on your device.

The single permission it asks for is VIBRATE, for the tap under the keys, and you can turn that
off.

NO ADS. NO IN-APP PURCHASES. NO SUBSCRIPTION. NO SIGN-IN.
```
